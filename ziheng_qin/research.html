Research

Efficient learning through better data and optimization.

Selected Projects

arXiv 2026

StateM

An agent-native runtime for harness scaling on Terminal-Bench 2.1. StateM organizes long-horizon execution around durable states, phase-local context, checked transitions, recoverable runbooks, and versioned procedural practices.

arXiv · Code

ICML 2025

Info-Coevolution

An efficient framework for data-model co-evolution. The method targets active learning settings where datasets grow over time and models must improve under realistic annotation and compute budgets.

Research collaboration between HPC-AI Lab and TikTok; accepted to ICML 2025.

ICLR 2024 Oral

InfoBatch

A lossless training acceleration framework based on unbiased dynamic data pruning. It achieves practical savings across classification, segmentation, detection, vision pretraining, diffusion, and instruction tuning workloads.

ECCV 2024

Dataset Growth

An efficient online algorithm for data cleaning and selection. It maintains growing datasets with awareness of cleanliness, diversity, and sampling value for single-modal and multimodal training.

ICML 2025

MERIT

A maximum-normalized element-wise ratio optimizer for large-batch language model training, designed to improve stability and enable larger effective batch sizes.
