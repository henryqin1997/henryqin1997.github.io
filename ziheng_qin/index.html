Biography

I am a researcher working on agents, efficient deep learning, data-centric AI, and large-scale model training.

My research has led to work such as StateM, InfoBatch, Dataset Growth, Info-Coevolution, and MERIT, with research collaborations across Meta, TikTok, Ali Tongyi Lab, and Tencent Hunyuan Lab.

Projects

All research →

StateM

An agent-native runtime for harness scaling, organizing execution around durable states, phase-local context, checked transitions, recoverable runbooks, and versioned procedural practices.

InfoBatch

Lossless training speed-up through unbiased dynamic data pruning, with practical savings across classification, segmentation, detection, vision pretraining, diffusion, and instruction tuning.

Info-Coevolution

An efficient active learning and data-model co-evolution framework for growing datasets and improving models under realistic compute and annotation budgets.

Dataset Growth

An online algorithm for data cleaning and selection that maintains growing datasets with awareness of cleanliness, diversity, and sampling value.
